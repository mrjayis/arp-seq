Arpeggiator

Sequencer

Each arpeggiated note takes the next step: its pitch is shifted by the step's semitones and its volume scaled by the step's level. A step switched Off strikes nothing, but a tail still ringing from the note before slides to the next arp note plus its interval.

Arp Voice · Oscillator 1

West-coast style, monophonic: a sine core folded by Timbre, blended by Shape with a second wave, into a low-pass gate. Combo strikes the gate: Cutoff sets brightness, loudness and how long it rings. VCA does the same without the filter. LP holds the note for the arp Gate time.

Envelope Applies to Oscillator 1 and 2.

Arp Voice · Oscillator 2

A second monophonic arp voice on every arpeggiated note. It plays each sequencer step's offset as-is (Same) or flipped (Inverted: +7 becomes −7). Sync cutoff lets Oscillator 1's Cutoff drive both gates.

Sequence
Mod → Osc 1 Works with Osc 2 switched Off, too: it becomes a silent modulator.

Progression

Program up to 8 chords; each plays for its number of beats, then the next, looping. The arpeggiator and chord rhythm play whatever chord is up; keys you hold are added to it.

Chord Rhythm

Repeats the held chord in a Euclidean rhythm (or holds it as a pad) while the arpeggiator keeps playing. Hits are spread as evenly as possible over the Steps (3 in 8 = x..x..x.).

Mono Synth · 101-style

Arm Write and play notes on the Keyboard (on screen, computer keys or MIDI): each one fills the next step. Rest adds silence, Tie holds the previous note, and with Legato on, the next notes slide in without retriggering. Click a step to write from there. While Write is armed, the arpeggiator is left alone.

Percussion

Pattern

Synthesized Latin, 808-style and Linn-style kits, or Your samples: load your own recordings (they stay in this browser). In time with the clock and swing. Tap a cell to cycle off → hit → accent; tap a name to mute it. Conditions make hits play only on some bars; a row's length (right) lets it loop sooner and drift against the others.

Breakbeat

Pattern

A drum break cut into slices and re-sequenced, tracker style. Select a row, then click a slice in the waveform to put it there. An empty row lets the last slice keep playing. C is the chance the row plays; R splits the step into a roll (R regular, Rv/RV volume down/up, Rn/RN note down/up, RR random notes). Your own break stays in this browser.

Song

Chain pattern slots A–D, one per bar, so the drums and break move through sections instead of looping one bar. Click a bar to change its slot; × removes it. Edit each slot with the A–D buttons in the Percussion and Breakbeat windows.
