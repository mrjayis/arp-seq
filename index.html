Progression

Program up to 8 chords; each plays for its number of beats, then the next, looping. The arpeggiator and chord rhythm play whatever chord is up; keys you hold are added to it.

Chord Rhythm

Repeats the held chord in a Euclidean rhythm while the arpeggiator keeps playing. Hits are spread as evenly as possible over the Steps (3 in 8 = x..x..x.).

Percussion

Vintage rhythm-box style Latin percussion, in time with the clock and swing. Tap a cell to cycle off → hit → accent; tap a name to mute it.

Sequencer

Each arpeggiated note takes the next step: its pitch is shifted by the step's semitones and its volume scaled by the step's level. A step switched Off strikes nothing, but a tail still ringing from the note before slides to the next arp note plus its interval.

Arp Voice · Oscillator 1

West-coast style, monophonic: a sine core folded by Timbre, blended by Shape with a second wave, into a low-pass gate. Combo strikes the gate: Cutoff sets brightness, loudness and how long it rings. VCA does the same without the filter. LP holds the note for the arp Gate time.

Arp Voice · Oscillator 2

A second monophonic arp voice on every arpeggiated note. It plays each sequencer step's offset as-is (Same) or flipped (Inverted: +7 becomes −7). Sync cutoff lets Oscillator 1's Cutoff drive both gates.

Sequence
